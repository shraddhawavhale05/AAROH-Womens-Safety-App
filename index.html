🛡️ Personal Safety Tips

  • Keep your phone charged whenever possible.
  • Save trusted emergency contacts in advance.
  • Share your journey details with someone you trust.
  • If you are in immediate danger in India, call 112 if safe to do so.
  • Remember that location and message delivery may fail if your device has no signal or internet access.
